Bill.com Upload Prep

Make an AI Club Reimbursement Bill.com-ready

Turn a member's SBC Reimbursement / Invoice Form PDF into one file which requires no additional steps in Bill.com other than uploading.

Saving the finished PDF isn't available in this view of the page. Use the website version instead, which saves normally: samantha-flaim.github.io/billcom-upload-prep

  1. 1Add the PDF Add the PDF. Drop in the PDF the member sent you: the SBC form plus receipts, as one file. The tool reads the WGA form and checks it against the receipts. The PDF never leaves your computer.
  2. 2Review and add a description Review. Fix anything flagged in red, pick any missing club or account from the WGA lists, type a short bill description, and check the approval note.
  3. 3Download and upload to Bill.com Upload. Download the new PDF and upload it in Bill.com's Enter a bill on the same day (the invoice and due dates are set to the day you download). Bill.com fills in the vendor, invoice number, dates, amount, and each line's account and class. Check the bill description and Notes on this bill, then click Create bill.
